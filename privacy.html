Privacy Policy

Gimakhin Uploader · Gimakhin Trick

1. What we access

When the channel owner authorizes Gimakhin Uploader with TikTok, the App receives an access token and refresh token, and uses these scopes only:

  • user.info.basic: to confirm which account is connected (open ID, display name, avatar).
  • video.upload / video.publish: to upload and publish the channel's own videos with their captions.

The App does not read followers, messages, comments, analytics or any data about other TikTok users.

2. How data is stored

Tokens are stored only in a local configuration file on the channel owner's own computer. We do not run a server, database or analytics for the App, and we do not collect data from viewers.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform the video is being published to (e.g. TikTok), as required to publish it.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use and are deleted from the local file when no longer needed. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. To ask about or request deletion of any data, email us at the address below.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children and is used only by the adult channel owner.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
