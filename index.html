Gimakhin Trick (기막힌 트릭)

Gimakhin Trick is a short-form science channel. Each episode uses AI-generated 3D visualizations to show the hidden design of everyday objects, such as why a microwave door has a dotted metal mesh.

매일 쓰는 물건 속, 아무도 몰랐던 설계를 3D 단면으로 보여주는 과학 쇼츠 채널입니다.

Gimakhin Uploader

Gimakhin Uploader is the channel's internal publishing tool. After the channel owner finishes an episode, the tool uploads the video file and its caption to the channel's own accounts through each platform's official API (TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram API, Threads API). It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, and does not offer accounts or services to other users.
